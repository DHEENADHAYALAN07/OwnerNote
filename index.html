Questions owners ask.

Short answers. Write to us for anything else.

Does Kai save anything without asking me?

No. Kai shows a card with the person, the amount and the date, and saves only when you tap Confirm. The one exception is Automatic UPI Notes on the app you mark as your shop's: that UPI money goes into the Daily Cash Note by itself, and one tap on Kai's notification removes it.

How can I speak to Kai?

You can use voice or text. Kai prepares a clear entry for you to review before you confirm it.

Which UPI apps does Automatic UPI Notes read?

Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BharatPe and BHIM — only after you turn it on and give notification access, and only the amount and the payer's name.

Where is my data kept?

Customers, suppliers, credit, stock and the Daily Cash Note are saved on OwnerNote's server after you confirm them, so they are there when you sign in again.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on the delete account page. You do not need to open the app.
